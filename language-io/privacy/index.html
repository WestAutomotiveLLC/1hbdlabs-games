Language.io: privacy & support

Last updated November 19, 2026

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email 1HBDLabs@gmail.com and include your device and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

  • Change the language: on the start screen, pick "I speak" and "I'm learning". Each language pair keeps its own lesson progress.
  • Lessons: answer a word right twice in duels to learn it. Learn all 10 words in a lesson to open the next one.
  • Practice: tap "Practice words" on the start screen for flashcards.

Privacy policy

Language.io is made by 1HBDLabs. We do not ask for an account or email, and we do not sell personal information. The name you type is only shown in your own game.

What stays on your device

Your name, chosen languages, lesson progress and sound setting are saved only on your device (in the app, or in your browser on the website). Deleting the app or clearing the browser's site data deletes them.

Advertising

The free phone app shows a short ad before each game through Google AdMob. Before ads load, the app asks for your consent where the law requires it. On iPhone the app does not ask for your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. Google may use approximate location from your IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. If you don't give consent, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalized. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. The website version shows no ads.

Children

Language.io is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Contact us at 1HBDLabs@gmail.com.
