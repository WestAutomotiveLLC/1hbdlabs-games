Language.io

Eat or get eaten. Every bite is a word duel in the language you're learning.

Lesson 1

  • Steer with your mouse or finger. Eat floating letters to grow.
  • Bump any blob to start a one-word duel. Whoever answers first wins.
  • The winner takes up to their own size from the loser. Run out of size and you're eaten, so big blobs are deadly. Big losers barely get knocked back, so small blobs can hit them again.
  • Blobs near you show what a duel is worth: green is what you'd win, red is what you'd lose.
  • Small blobs can hide in thornbushes. A big blob can smash a bush to expose who's hiding, but it cracks open: it loses 20% of its size, slows down, and loses more in duels for a few seconds. Bushes grow back after 40 seconds.

Practice

Space flips the card. Left arrow is Again, right arrow is Got it. Practice doesn't change your lesson progress; duels do.
