Tomato Escape: privacy & support

Last updated November 4, 2026

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email 1HBDLabs@gmail.com and include your phone model and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

  • Change the language: on the main menu, tap a flag under LEARN, or tap DIRECTION to switch which way the words go. Each language keeps its own progress.
  • Easy or Hard: Easy shows four answers at every gate; Hard has you type the translation (accents don't count).
  • Watch the story again: tap STORY on the main menu.

Privacy policy

Tomato Escape is made by 1HBDLabs. We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not sell personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your level progress, chosen language, Easy/Hard setting and music setting are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The free game shows ads through Google AdMob. Before ads load, the game asks for your consent where the law requires it, and on iPhone it asks before using the advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). With your permission, Google may use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location from your IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. If you say no, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalized. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

Tomato Escape is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Contact us at 1HBDLabs@gmail.com.
